# IHK (Industrie- und Handelskammer, Germany's regional chamber of commerce)

Quasi-public regional body representing commercial businesses
(Gewerbebetriebe). Mandatory membership for anyone registering a Gewerbe.
79 regional IHKs nationwide. Issues Tragfähigkeitsbescheinigung for
Gründungszuschuss applications.

Important limitation: Freiberufler (§ 18 EStG) are NOT IHK members; they
belong to their respective professional chamber instead.
