## Structure

- `Personal/`: notes from the former Documents main vault.
- `wiki/`: the Claude Obsidian compounding wiki.
- `Local Knowledge/`: SwarmVault inbox, graph, retrieval, and generated digests.
- `Projects/`: active project knowledge.
- `Archive/`: preserved legacy vault material.

## Rules

- Do not create another general-purpose Obsidian vault.
- Place unattended imports in `Local Knowledge/inbox/`.
- Keep source material immutable after ingestion.
- Review candidate and contradiction queues.
- Never digitize private spiritual content such as awrad, wirds, or teacher instructions.
