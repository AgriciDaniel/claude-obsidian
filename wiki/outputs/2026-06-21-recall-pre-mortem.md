# Recall pre-mortem

## Decision

Conditional go for a 30-90 day paid validation sprint. No-go for another broad feature cycle or scaling the current agency SaaS claims.

Recall is worth testing for boutique social and creative agencies with 5-20 staff that produce frequent client briefs and already lose useful references across personal saves, Slack, Notion, slides and swipe files.

The narrow promise is:

> Turn the social references a strategy team deliberately saves into one source-cited creative brief for a live client.

## Why Recall fails in the pre-mortem

Recall changes from a consumer second brain into an agency platform before validating a recurring, budgeted job. The product markets collaboration, client separation, cited briefs and outcome learning while the implementation remains owner-scoped. Teams like the demonstration but do not establish a repeated capture habit. Founder-assisted imports and manual boards consume too much time. Existing tools remain good enough. The Execution Router attracts a different audience and distracts from the evidence-to-brief workflow. Trust and commercial inconsistencies delay procurement. Customers use Recall for occasional projects, export the result, and churn.

## Highest risks

1. The problem is inconvenient rather than urgent.
2. The subscription value depends on team and client features that are not live.
3. The buyer and budget owner are unclear.
4. Team activation requires substantial behavior change.
5. The output is episodic and lacks a recurring outcome loop.
6. Foreplay, Notion, Slack, Instagram collections and slides are adequate substitutes.
7. Capture is fragile and Instagram Business Discovery does not import saved folders.
8. The Execution Router is disconnected from Recall evidence and the agency ICP.
9. Trust is weakened by stale strategy, legal and pricing claims.
10. Founder-supported pilots may become an unprofitable service.

## Strongest thesis

The potential moat is not capture, AI summaries, search or boards. It is the private chain:

source → who noticed it → why it mattered → client → recommendation → delivered work → result

That chain is currently a strategy concept, not a complete product capability.

## What can be sold now

A standardized concierge engagement:

- one agency;
- one live client brief;
- 50-150 relevant references;
- founder-assisted import and organization;
- one source-backed creative direction board delivered within 48 hours;
- observation of its use in a client or internal decision;
- £500 paid upfront.

Do not sell the current product as a complete agency intelligence operating system.

## Product direction

Build only the validated chain:

1. Explicit capture.
2. Required reason-saved annotation and client assignment.
3. Pattern supported by at least three sources.
4. Editable source-cited brief.
5. Delivered-work and outcome record.

Keep the Execution Router secondary until it routes tasks directly from evidence-backed briefs. Otherwise separate it as a free microtool.

## 30-day evidence test

- Complete 8-10 screen-shared workflow interviews.
- Make 75-100 targeted contacts.
- Sell at least two identical £500 pilots.
- Deliver first useful output within 48 hours and no more than five founder hours.
- Have both outputs used in real client work.
- Demonstrate at least two to three hours saved or a material improvement in client confidence.
- Obtain at least one written commitment to continue at £149/month or more.

## Kill or pivot conditions

Stop or narrow the agency thesis if:

- 100 qualified contacts produce fewer than five calls or two paid pilots;
- fewer than 20% of observed agencies share the same recurring problem;
- teams cannot supply 50 relevant references;
- fewer than two team members contribute;
- outputs are not used or reused in real client work;
- no customer accepts at least £149/month after completed paid pilots;
- founder support exceeds five hours per customer per month;
- reliable capture requires prohibited scraping or continual DOM repair.

## Pricing conclusions

- £500 is credible for a fixed deliverable, not an open-ended product-development pilot.
- Cap founder effort and do not credit the full fee; a maximum 50% conversion credit is safer.
- £149/month becomes credible after repeated collaboration saves measurable time.
- £349/month requires client isolation, permissions and recurring cited outputs.
- Remove £749 from active selling until governance demand is observed.
- Correct the current credit wording: three Studio months total £447, so a £500 full credit cannot be applied as stated.

## Immediate contradictions to fix

- Vault consumer positioning conflicts with the agency product.
- The Router is prominent despite strategy stating Recall cannot win as a generic router.
- Paid team capabilities are not represented in the current schema.
- Pilot enquiry depends on `hello@userecall.app`; verify the domain and mailbox before outreach.
- Instagram import is implemented but requires Meta credentials.
- CI and production trust checks need broader coverage before paid agency data is accepted.

## Bottom line

The idea is worth a disciplined commercial experiment because the human-judgment and provenance thesis is differentiated enough to test. It is not yet proven enough to justify building the full agency platform.

The next milestone is not another feature. It is two agencies paying for the same evidence-to-brief outcome and one agreeing to recurring pricing.
