# Recall — Product Strategy

Formerly: Saved Brain + OpenFlowKit merger.

## Product identity
- **Name:** Recall
- **Tagline:** "Capture everything. Find anything. Think faster."
- **Description:** Your AI-powered knowledge layer — save from social, capture with voice, find anything with semantic search.
- **Domain:** userecall.app (or recall.so / getrecall.ai)
- **Repo:** github.com/manazoid4/recall (renamed from saved-brain)

## Thesis
Every strong project in this portfolio = AI signal recovery for underserved communities. Recall is the consumer flagship of that thesis.

## What merged
- **Saved Brain** (production-ready) — social saves → AI knowledge base
- **OpenFlowKit** (voice pipeline) — voice dictation → refined text → knowledge base

## Pricing
- Free: 50 items, text search
- Pro: £8/mo or £49 lifetime — semantic search, AI enrichment, voice capture
- Teams: £19/seat/mo — shared boards, team knowledge graph, company brain

## Portfolio decisions
| Product | Decision |
|---|---|
| OpenFlowKit | Folded into Recall as voice capture layer |
| SecureShift | Keep separate brand, share agent/DB infrastructure with JobFilterV1 |
| JobFilterV1 | Keep separate brand, share infrastructure with SecureShift |
| khutba.io | Keep totally separate — community/faith tech, Ramadan 2027 |
| AgentDock | Mothball — needs enterprise sales motion not built yet |
| InkWeave | **Archived** — no code, crowded market, no edge |
| LimitLens | **Archived** — tiny market, providers ship this natively |

## Remaining build priorities (Recall)
1. OpenFlowKit voice capture integration (Capture tab)
2. Teams/shared boards (B2B ARR lever)
3. Mobile app (voice capture on mobile = moat vs Readwise/Matter)
4. Obsidian/Notion two-way sync
5. Chrome extension connect-account flow
